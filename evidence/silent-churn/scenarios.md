## Design Rule

The set includes benign cases so the participant cannot succeed by treating every weak signal as churn.

## Hidden-Key Rule

When used with a participant, do not show the expected disposition until the run is complete.

## Boundary

The expected dispositions are experimental scoring keys, not validated commercial thresholds.
