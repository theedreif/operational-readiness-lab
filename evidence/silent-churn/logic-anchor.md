# Silent Churn Logic Anchor

## Trigger

Weak relationship signals diverge from apparently healthy headline account metrics.

## Filter

Inspect:

- normal seasonality;
- stakeholder role changes;
- known scheduling constraints;
- product usage trend;
- support history;
- commercial calendar;
- prior account behavior;
- communication response pattern;
- meeting participation;
- whether multiple weak signals form a pattern or remain isolated noise.

## Anchor

Experimental disposition:

- **MAINTAIN**
- **INVESTIGATE**
- **ESCALATE**
- **INTERVENE**

The evidence required for each real-world action must come from the accountable organization's validated rules.

If no authoritative threshold exists:

**UNKNOWN / REQUIRES AUTHORITATIVE VERIFICATION**

## Core Judgment

**Is the account healthy, or is the dashboard lagging the relationship?**
