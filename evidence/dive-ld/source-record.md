# Dive-LD Source Record

**Source record date:** 2026-09-20

## Source 1 — Reuters

**Title:** Iran says it captured US submarine drone in Strait of Hormuz  
**Published:** 2026-09-08  
**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/iran-says-it-captured-us-submarine-drone-strait-hormuz-2026-09-08/

Reuters reported that Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they captured a U.S. uncrewed submarine near the Strait of Hormuz and that Iranian media identified it as an Anduril Dive-LD. Reuters reported a Pentagon acknowledgment of the loss and a U.S. account that the older vehicle had malfunctioned more than a day earlier and did not contain sensitive data or classified sonar/radar equipment.

## Source 2 — DefenseScoop

**Title:** U.S. military's Anduril Dive-LD drone was 'dead in the water' before Iran nabbed it  
**Published:** 2026-09-08  
**URL:** https://defensescoop.com/2026/09/08/us-militarys-anduril-dive-ld-drone-captured-by-iran/

DefenseScoop quoted CENTCOM spokesperson Capt. Tim Hawkins saying the vehicle had been malfunctioning well before recovery and was effectively dead in the water for more than a day. The report also quoted Anduril describing Dive-LD as attritable and designed for environments where vehicle loss is an expected possibility.

## Provenance Rule

Treat contested descriptions such as "capture," "seizure," and the exact mechanism of recovery as attributed claims unless independently established. Preserve dated source states rather than silently reconciling them.
