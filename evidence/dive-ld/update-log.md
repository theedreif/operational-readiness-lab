## v1.0 — Deep Evidence Pack

### Failure Mode
A degraded, unresponsive, or lost autonomous platform becomes a recovery, exposure, mission-continuity, handoff, and decision-authority problem.

### Source Evidence
Recent public reporting was separated into a dated Source Record and Claim → Evidence Map.

### Judgment
**When does an anomaly stop being a vehicle problem and become a different decision regime?**

### Tool
Logic Anchor + Friction Matrix + Telemetry Plan.

### Friction
Contact, telemetry quality, elapsed time, handoff, recoverability, authority, competing mission demand, exposure, and ambiguous system state.

### Evidence
The vehicle loss/recovery is **PUBLICLY REPORTED**. The Lab instrument remains **ILLUSTRATIVE / NOT YET RUN**.

### Update
The narrative case has become a bounded rehearsal package without inventing internal procedures, thresholds, recovery attempts, or causal explanations.

### Next Evidence Event
