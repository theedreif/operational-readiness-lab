# Independent Supervisor Review — DFR Triage

You are being asked to review a synthetic decision instrument, not endorse a product, vendor, agency, or field-readiness claim.

## What you will review

Twelve short synthetic DFR triage scenarios. Each asks whether an automated recommendation should be:

**ACCEPTED · INSPECTED · ESCALATED · REJECTED**

## What we need from you

For each scenario, make the judgment before seeing the experiment's expected answer. Identify the cue that mattered, missing information, confidence, and whether the scenario is clear enough to score.

Disagreement is welcome. If the scenario, Logic Anchor, or expected answer is weak, say so.

## What this review means

Your review will be recorded as **SUPERVISOR CALIBRATION** evidence only. It will not be represented as validation of operational readiness or field effectiveness.

## Reviewer independence

Please disclose whether you helped design the experiment, have already seen its scoring key, or have another relationship that could affect independence.

**The goal is not agreement. The goal is an instrument that survives expert scrutiny.**
