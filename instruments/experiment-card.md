# Experiment Card

## Failure Mode
What materially consequential failure are we trying to make observable?

## Signal
What behavior, event, state, delay, or mismatch reveals the failure forming?

## Threshold
What boundary changes the required action?

If no authoritative threshold exists, write:

**UNKNOWN / REQUIRES AUTHORITATIVE VERIFICATION**

## Friction
What realistic variation will make the operator or system discriminate rather than merely repeat?

## Red
What observable condition means the instrument has detected the target failure mode?

## Evidence
What will be recorded? Include timestamps, decisions, confidence, recovery behavior, and relevant system state.

## Update
What doctrine, workflow, scenario, threshold, or control should change if the evidence persists?

## Evidence Status
Choose one: **ILLUSTRATIVE / SIMULATED / PUBLICLY REPORTED / FIELD-OBSERVED / VALIDATED**

## Guardrail
A scenario result is not automatically a field-readiness claim. State what the evidence supports and what remains unknown.
