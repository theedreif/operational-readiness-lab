# Logic Anchor

A Logic Anchor encodes the minimum expert judgment required to recognize a consequential state and act within an authoritative boundary.

```
TRIGGER → FILTER → ANCHOR
```

## Trigger
What observable condition should attract attention?

## Filter
What information distinguishes the dangerous condition from noise, a false positive, or a benign variation?

## Anchor
What authoritative limit, rule, policy, physical constraint, or decision boundary governs action?

Never invent a safety threshold, operating limit, policy, or regulatory requirement.

If the anchor is not established:

**UNKNOWN / REQUIRES AUTHORITATIVE VERIFICATION**

The AI may create conditions around the anchor. It does not get to invent the anchor.
