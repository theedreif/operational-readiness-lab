# Supervisor Calibration Protocol v1.0

**Purpose:** introduce independent expert judgment between loop evidence and stronger readiness claims.

Supervisor Calibration asks whether an independent reviewer agrees with the experiment's predeclared judgment logic — and, more importantly, where and why they disagree.

## Reviewer Qualification

Record only observable qualifications relevant to the case:

- domain / role;
- years or level of relevant experience, if the reviewer chooses to provide it;
- relationship to the experiment: independent / internal / author / participant;
- conflicts or prior exposure to the scoring key.

Do not convert credentials into automatic correctness.

## Blind Review Rule

Where practical, the reviewer receives:

1. the scenario or case evidence;
2. the available cues;
3. the proposed decision choices;
4. the evidence boundary.

Withhold the predeclared expected disposition until the reviewer commits a judgment.

## Calibration Record

For every reviewed item capture:

- reviewer ID;
- case and scenario ID;
- reviewer disposition;
- reviewer confidence;
- cues considered decisive;
- missing information;
- whether the decision boundary is clear;
- proposed alternative disposition, if any;
- rationale;
- agreement/disagreement with the predeclared key after reveal;
- whether disagreement indicates a reviewer difference, ambiguous scenario, weak Logic Anchor, or invalid scoring key.

## Disagreement Is Evidence

Do not treat disagreement as reviewer failure.

Classify it:

- **CALIBRATED AGREEMENT** — independent judgment supports the predeclared key for substantially the same reason;
- **REASONABLE ALTERNATIVE** — another disposition is defensible from the supplied evidence;
- **SCENARIO AMBIGUITY** — wording/evidence does not support a unique judgment;
- **ANCHOR DEFECT** — Logic Anchor fails to distinguish the consequential state;
- **KEY DEFECT** — expected disposition is not adequately supported;
- **AUTHORITY GAP** — correct decision depends on an authoritative rule not supplied.

## Promotion Rule

Supervisor agreement does not validate field readiness.

It can support the narrower claim that the instrument's judgment logic is independently interpretable and calibrated enough to proceed to stronger comparison.

> **Calibration is not consensus. It is inspectable disagreement.**
