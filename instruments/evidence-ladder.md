# Evidence Ladder

Readiness claims should become stronger only as the evidence becomes stronger.

```
LOOP EVIDENCE
      ↓
SUPERVISOR CALIBRATION
      ↓
NEAR-MISS COMPARISON
      ↓
FIELD OUTCOME CORRELATION
      ↓
VALIDATED READINESS CLAIM
```

## Rule

Do not promote a claim because a simulation looked predictive.

**Earn the claim in arrears.**

Synthetic evidence can validate an instrument's behavior. It cannot, by itself, establish field effectiveness.
